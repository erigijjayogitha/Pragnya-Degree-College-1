About Our College

Pragnya Degree & P.G. College is a private degree college located in Chandanagar, Hyderabad, Telangana It is one of the private degree-college options in Chandanagar.
Its courses cover commerce, management and computer science. Check the current placement record, faculty, fees and facilities before taking admission..

History Of Pragnya Degree College

Pragnya Degree & P.G. College was established in the year 2010 in Chandanagar, Hyderabad, Telangana.
The main aim of the institution was to provide quality higher education at an affordable cost to students and help them achieve their goals.
The college was established with a vision of spreading knowledge, developing skills and creating better career opportunities for students.
